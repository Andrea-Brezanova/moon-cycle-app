# Legal drafts (not published)

These are **drafts for the owner and a qualified legal adviser**. They are not legal advice, have not been reviewed by a lawyer, are not linked from the app and are not hosted anywhere. Text in `[SQUARE BRACKETS]` is a decision or fact only the owner can supply. Nothing here may be published, or the in-app links (`AppLinks.privacyPolicy`, `terms`, `dataUse`) filled in, without the owner's approval.

- `privacy-policy-draft.md`: written from the app's verified data practices (`docs/privacy-and-data-audit.md`, `test/privacy/`).
- `terms-of-use-draft.md`
- `apple-privacy-manifest-and-export-compliance.md`: audit of the Apple privacy manifest and the export-compliance answer.

**Keep in step with the code.** The policy is accurate only while these facts hold; each is checked by a test, so a change should fail CI before the policy goes stale: no accounts, no server, no analytics/advertising SDKs, crash reporting off, external recipe lookups debug-only, cloud generation unwired.
