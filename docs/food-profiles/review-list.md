# Prioritised review list and sampling sheets

Order: (1) blocked foods needing an owner decision, (2) categories with the highest number of risk flags, (3) within a category, highest-risk foods first. A reviewer who checks the **sample** of each category (the top-risk 4 plus every 10th food) and finds no problem can spot-check the rest; any problem in a sample means checking the whole category.

## 1. Owner decisions (blocked)

- [ ] **Alcohol (moderate)**: 'Alcohol (moderate)' is not a nutrient-offering food, and any profile would be a health-risk message that needs expert/editorial wording (Dietary Guidelines, NIAAA). Needs an owner decision on whether to profile it at all.
- [ ] **Bancha tea**: Brewed tea has almost no nutrients in USDA records and any caffeine/fluoride/tannin message needs a sourced caution. Needs an editorial decision on format.
- [ ] **Black soybean**: No USDA record for black soybeans. The yellow soybean record (174271) is a different variety and was not substituted without owner approval.
- [ ] **Coffee**: Brewed coffee has almost no nutrients in USDA records; caffeine guidance (FDA, pregnancy) needs sourced wording that was not read for this project. Needs an editorial decision.
- [ ] **Decaf coffee**: As coffee: almost no nutrients; residual caffeine and sourcing need an editorial decision.
- [ ] **Dulse**: No USDA FoodData Central record for dulse (searched SR Legacy and Foundation). Needs a verified composition source before a profile.
- [ ] **Fresh-water clam**: USDA has only marine/mixed clam records; freshwater clam composition differs. The catalogue marks it not ship-ready.
- [ ] **Guacamole**: No USDA SR Legacy record for guacamole; composition varies by recipe (avocado, onion, lime, salt). Needs a defined recipe or a branded product.
- [ ] **Hijiki**: No USDA record for hijiki. Hijiki also has a known inorganic-arsenic concern raised by food-safety agencies; a sourced caution (not read for this project) would be needed before any profile.
- [ ] **Kombu**: No USDA record for kombu (dried kelp). Raw kelp (168457) exists and is drafted as 'kelp', but it is a different preparation (raw vs dried) and was not used as a stand-in. Iodine content is very high and needs a sourced caution.
- [ ] **Muesli**: No generic USDA record for muesli (only brand cereals such as Alpen and Familia). Needs a defined product.
- [ ] **Oat milk**: No USDA SR Legacy record for oat milk; composition is brand-specific (fortification, added oil, sugar). Needs a defined product or label data.
- [ ] **Salt**: Not a nutrient-offering food: salt supplies sodium (and iodine only if iodised), and sodium guidance (e.g. the Dietary Guidelines limit) is a safety/limit message, not a 'what this food offers' profile. Needs an editorial decision on format before any draft.
- [ ] **Seitan**: No USDA record for seitan. Values depend on the product (vital wheat gluten content, added soy sauce, salt). Needs a defined product.
- [ ] **Soft-shell crab**: Soft-shell crab is blue crab caught after moulting and eaten whole, usually fried. USDA has cooked blue crab (174205) but not soft-shell crab, so the preparation and whole-animal composition differ. Needs a decision on whether blue crab may stand in.
