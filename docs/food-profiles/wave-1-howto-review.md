## A. Proposed wording edits (reviewer decides)

| Food | Issue | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Bean sprouts (`bean-sprouts`) | Suggests raw sprouts in salads and spring rolls; the caution (FDA: avoid raw sprouts if at greater risk of foodborne illness) is separate and easy to miss. | Cook sprouts thoroughly if you are pregnant, older or have a weakened immune system. Add them to stir-fries and noodle dishes at the end of cooking, or to salads and spring rolls if you are not at greater risk. |
| Green pea (`green-pea`) | "or eat from the freezer, lightly cooked" is ambiguous and can read as eating frozen peas without cooking. | Replace with: "...or cook from frozen until hot through." |
| Haddock (`haddock`) | "poach in milk" brings in milk, but the profile carries only the fish allergen caution. | Bake or poach in stock or water, pan-fry, or use in fish pie and chowder. (If milk is kept, say "milk (dairy allergen)".) |
| Mackerel (`mackerel`) | "serve smoked in salads and on toast" suggests ready-to-eat smoked fish. Refrigerated smoked fish is a listeria concern for people at greater risk (FDA advice for pregnancy); the shown serving is cooked, not smoked. | Grill, bake or pan-fry with lemon. (Remove the smoked suggestion, or add that smoked fish is not advised if you are at greater risk of foodborne illness.) |
