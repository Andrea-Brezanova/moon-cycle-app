# Draft batch 7: meat, eggs, soy, dairy and ferments (21 foods; nothing here is published)

beef, tempeh, chicken, eggs, tofu, pork, lamb, turkey; feta, plain yogurt, cottage cheese,
kefir, ricotta, goat cheese, Greek yogurt, mozzarella, Swiss cheese; miso, sauerkraut,
pickles, kimchi. USDA records read 27 Oct 2026. With this batch every one of the 142
phase-assigned foods has either an approved profile (5) or a draft (137).

## Identity questions
- **Beef, pork, lamb, chicken, turkey:** the catalogue entries are generic; each record is one
  representative lean cut, named in the profile (90% lean ground beef; roasted lean pork loin;
  roasted lean leg of lamb; skinless chicken breast; roasted turkey breast). Other cuts and
  processed meats differ a lot.
- **Kefir:** the only USDA kefir records are branded (Lifeway); the profile says so.
- **Greek yogurt:** USDA lists no household measure for this record. 170 g (a 6-ounce
  container) is my choice. Plain yogurt and Greek yogurt are the whole-milk records.
- **Tofu:** calcium depends on the coagulant (calcium sulfate here).
- **Sauerkraut, pickles, kimchi:** canned and pasteurised or vinegar-based foods are not
  live-fermented; nutrient records do not capture live cultures, and no probiotic claim is made.

## Cautions
- Cooking temperatures, USDA FSIS Safe Minimum Internal Temperature Chart (read 27 Oct 2026):
  poultry 165 °F; steaks, chops and roasts 145 °F with a 3-minute rest, ground meat 160 °F;
  eggs 160 °F.
- Existing catalogue cautions are carried over (soy, egg, dairy, "high in salt", kimchi fish
  sauce label check). They have no external source recorded.
- Sodium is very high in miso, pickles, sauerkraut, kimchi and feta; it is not in the key
  list and no sourced salt caution has been added (no sodium reference was read this session).

## Unsourced wording to accept, change or remove
Cooking tips, "mellows its flavour", "takes on the flavour", and a few flavour words.
