# Product opportunities (analysis, 1 Nov 2026)

Read from the current app and code, not from user data (there is none). Ranked by value to the user, effort, uncertainty and impact. Nothing here is built or approved.

| # | Opportunity | User value | Effort | Uncertainty | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Finish Build a Meal's journey**: "goes well with" suggestions with a plain reason, then a one-tap "save this meal" and "log it" | High | Medium (foundation exists, `docs/build-a-meal-2.md`) | Medium | Build a Meal ends at a recipe list. The missing step is completing and keeping a meal. |
| 2 | **A "today" Home that answers one question**: what to eat, one movement idea, one thing to log, using cycle context only as a label | High | Medium | Medium | Home shows the phase well; make the next action obvious without crowding. |
| 3 | **Find food and recipes faster**: search that spans foods, recipes and my recipes; recent and favourite foods; filters by diet and time | High | Medium | Low | Search lives on "All foods"; recipes and foods have separate entry points. |
| 4 | **Understand nutrition**: publish the approved Food Detail profiles (98 staged) with "what this food offers" and a plain serving | High | Low (content approval, not code) | Low | The profile page is the strongest differentiator and is blocked only on review and approval. |
| 5 | **Rediscover saved content**: one "My things" view (saved recipes, links, routines) with recent items first and simple tags | Medium | Medium | Low | Collections exist; finding a saved item again takes several taps. |
| 6 | **Meaningful logging and review**: a weekly summary of meals and symptoms the user logged (counts and patterns only, no advice) | Medium | Medium | Medium | The diary captures data but returns nothing. Privacy-safe, on device. |
| 7 | Reminders that earn their place: choose which reminder types; a gentle "log today?" nudge | Medium | Low | Low | Reminders exist; options are few. |
| 8 | Onboarding that shows value in two minutes: skip-able cycle questions, then a first meal idea | Medium | Medium | Medium | Reduces drop-off before the first useful moment. |
| 9 | Shopping list from a built meal or a recipe in one tap, grouped by aisle (exists) with check-off memory | Medium | Low | Low | Close to done; tidy the hand-off. |
| 10 | Accessibility polish: section jump links on long pages, VoiceOver pass | Medium | Low | Low | Reliability of trust. |

**Retention through utility, not streaks:** prefer features that complete a task (plan, shop, log) over gamification. **Keep the Home screen simple:** one primary card, then quiet sections; resist adding a card per feature.
**Evidence boundary:** every suggestion above avoids phase-specific nutrition claims; cycle context stays a label, not a reason.
