# Design deviations

Every place where the Flutter implementation differs from the Editorial v3 handoff (`design/`), or had to decide something the handoff doesn't specify. Anything marked **open** is waiting for a decision.

| # | Where | What the handoff says | What the app does | Why | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Buttons, round icon buttons, stepper ± at its limits | Disabled state not designed | 40% opacity, not tappable | Onboarding needs "Continue" disabled until a step is valid | approved 11 Oct 2026 (v1 default) |
| 2 | Everything tappable | No ripples; pressed states only for chips and food rows | Content dims to 70% while pressed; no Material ripples. Designed pressed states (chip, food row) are used where they exist | Touch feedback without visual clutter | approved 11 Oct 2026 |
| 3 | Onboarding links ("I'm not sure", "Skip for now", "I already have an account") | Spec §6: every text link has the 2 pt ochre underline. The approved onboarding screens show a thin underline in the text colour | **Intentional onboarding exception:** `TextLink.onboarding` is DM Sans 15 Medium with a 1 pt underline in the text colour (plum, or cream on the welcome screen). Everywhere else `TextLink` keeps the 2 pt ochre underline | The approved screens win over the generic rule | approved 11 Oct 2026 |
| 4 | Text-link underline position | CSS `text-underline-offset: 4px` | Drawn 4 pt below the alphabetic baseline | Flutter's underline can't be offset; visually equivalent | approved 11 Oct 2026 |
| 5 | DM Sans | Mock-ups use variable DM Sans with automatic optical size (9–40) | Static 14 pt optical-size cut at every size | Differences are only visible at 11–13 pt; not worth the complexity | approved 11 Oct 2026 |
| 6 | Fraunces | Mock-ups use continuous optical sizing | Two static cuts: 72 pt for 40 pt and up, 24 pt below | As specified in spec §4 | approved 11 Oct 2026 |
| 7 | Stepper value on narrow screens or at large text sizes | Not designed (390 pt, standard text size) | The value shrinks to fit only when it doesn't fit; unchanged at 390 pt | Prevents overflow at 320 pt and 1.5× text | approved 11 Oct 2026 |
| 8 | Button labels on narrow screens or at large text sizes | Not designed | The label wraps and the button grows taller (corner radius stays 28, so a one-line button is the designed 56 pt pill). At large text sizes the side padding tightens from 24 to 16 pt. Labels are **never truncated** | Important actions must stay understandable | approved 11 Oct 2026 |
| 9 | Chip labels on narrow screens or at large text sizes | Not designed | Chips are compact controls and may end with "…" when a label can't fit | A chip can't grow without breaking its row | approved 11 Oct 2026 |
| 10 | Tab bar labels at large text sizes | Not designed; the tab bar is a fixed 88 pt | Tab labels stay 11 pt and don't follow the system text size. The screen-reader labels are unaffected | Five labels can't grow in a fixed-height bar without clipping or overlapping. iOS's own tab bars handle this with a long-press magnifier, which is not in this design | **open** |
| 11 | Onboarding "Add another" (step 4) | Not drawn | A sheet in the system's sheet style: "Add a food to *avoid*", a search field over the catalogue, matching foods as hairline rows; "Add “…”" only when nothing matches. Chosen foods appear as selected chips (Menstrual tint with a check); tapping one removes it. The Allergies screen reuses it | Needed to make "Add another" work | **open** |
| 12 | Onboarding welcome, v1 | The approved screen shows "I already have an account" | The link is hidden (spec §12) but its space is kept, so the layout matches the approved screen | Hiding it would move the copy 29 pt down | **open** |
| 13 | Onboarding reveal when the period is already late | Not drawn | The last line reads "Period N days late" | Possible when the start date is long ago | **open** |
| 14 | Onboarding "I’m not sure" | Sets today − 7 | Also moves on to the next step | The user has answered the question | **open** |
| 15 | Welcome ring on short phones | 150 pt from the top at 844 pt | Moves up in proportion on shorter screens, so it never runs into the copy | Small phones | **open** |
| 16 | Home "Foods for today" | Six chips, one per category; the mock-up shows Barley, Artichoke, Avocado, **Green lentil**, Brazil nut, Chicken | The first food the user eats in each category, in the stored order (Black-eyed pea in follicular). The PRD only says "one per category"; the mock-up's choice isn't a rule | A rule that works for every phase and preference | **open** |
| 17 | Home, one or two days late | The late card shows from 3 days late | Days 1–2 late: no card yet, but the hero already reads "Period 1 day late · Day 29 in cycle" and the dot stays at the end | The other meta line ("Day 29 of 28") would be wrong | **open** |
| 18 | Log a period, date within 10 days of another period | Not drawn | Save is disabled with the line "That’s within 10 days of a period you’ve already logged. Choose its first day, or another date." | Plan §6.2 validation | **open** |
| 19 | Recipe photos | Mock-ups show photos | Tinted placeholders (spec §6 "No photo") until the 12 recovered photos are approved and bundled (`open-questions.md`) | No licensed photos in the app yet | **open** |
| 20 | Home shopping block with no recipe items | "8 to buy · 6 from recipes" | "8 to buy" alone when nothing comes from a recipe (and "0 to buy" when empty) | "· 0 from recipes" reads oddly | **open** |
| 21 | Orbit drag | "Dragging around the orbit" | A drag that starts on the ring previews; a drag starting on the moon's centre scrolls the page | Otherwise the page can't be scrolled from the hero | **open** |
| 22 | Recipes feature card meta | "Meals · Green lentil, broccoli, lemon" | The first three key ingredients ("Green lentil, broccoli, zucchini") | A rule for every recipe; the mock-up's pick isn't one | **open** |
| 23 | Recipes, an ingredient with no recipes (All meal types) | Not drawn | "No follicular recipes with *rye*", "None of this phase’s recipes uses rye yet." and "Clear ingredient" (same layout as `17-recipes-empty-ingredient`) | "Cook with…" lists foods with 0 recipes too | **open** |
| 24 | Recipe detail with avoided ingredients | Not drawn | The row says "You avoid this", the add button skips it, and a note reads "We’ll leave out tahini: it’s on your avoid list." | PRD §9. Rare: such recipes are hidden from the lists | **open** |
| 25 | Shop row tap target | The round checkbox (26 pt, 44 pt hit area) | The whole left of the row (checkbox, name, source) ticks the item; screen readers hear one checkbox with the name and source. × stays separate | A larger target, and the checkbox sits flush as drawn |  **open** |
| 26 | Shop, Done expanded | Done is drawn collapsed only | The same rows with a filled checkbox and the name in the muted colour; tapping one moves it back to To buy | Needed for "uncheck moves it back" (spec §8) | **open** |
| 27 | Shop add field | Not specified | Submitting keeps the keyboard up for the next item; tapping elsewhere puts it away (all text fields) | Fixes entries running together on iOS | **open** |
| 28 | Settings sub-screens: editing values | My Cycle's rows have chevrons; the editors aren't drawn | "Last period started" and History rows open the Log a period sheet as "Edit period" (start date, a Period length stepper, and "Delete this period" when it isn't the only one). Cycle length and Period length open a sheet with the onboarding steppers and copy ("Your rhythm", "How long is your cycle, *usually?*"), plus "Use my logged average" once there is enough history | The values must be editable | **open** |
| 29 | My Cycle history without an end date | Every drawn row has an end date | The title is the start date alone ("5 Oct") and the subtitle leaves out the days ("current cycle") until a length is set in Edit period | Not inventing a period length | **open** |
| 30 | Settings › Allergies search field | A search field over the food catalogue | A field-shaped button that opens the onboarding food search sheet (deviation 11); chosen foods appear as the removable chips | One search flow for onboarding and Settings | **open** |
| 31 | Notifications detail switches | All three drawn on | "When a new phase starts" and "Before your period" can't be changed while Reminders is off; tapping anywhere on a row flips its switch | The details only apply when reminders are on; larger targets | **open** |
| 32 | Contact support and the policy rows, before their addresses exist | Working links | "Contact support" shows the disabled state and the three policy rows do nothing until the addresses are set (`lib/app/config/app_links.dart`, `open-questions.md` 7) | No invented email addresses or URLs | **open** |
| 33 | Help answers | Only the first answer is drawn | "Is Moon Cycle a contraceptive…" and "Where is my data stored?" reuse the approved notes from My Cycle and Privacy & Data; "Why can’t I see some foods?" is a factual description of the filters; "What if my cycle is irregular?" shows "[Answer needs content review]" | Spec §11.5: answers need content review; no health content invented | **open** |
| 34 | Settings footer version | "Version [x.y]" | "Version 0.1.0" from the build (plus " · dev" in dev builds) | Real version; the flavour helps testers | **open** |
| 35 | Scrolling under the status bar (Foods, Recipes, Shop, Settings) | Static mock-ups | Scrolled content is clipped below the status bar (unchanged at rest) | Text ran under the clock on the simulator | **open** |
| 36 | Settings › Allergies root summary | "Peanuts, shellfish" while Allergies shows Mushrooms too | The row lists everything avoided ("Peanuts, shellfish, mushrooms"), or "None" | The summary must match the screen | **open** |
| 37 | Reminder permission | Plan §M14 mentions a permission priming screen; none is drawn | No extra screen: turning on Reminders shows the system prompt. If it's refused, Reminders stays off and a short message says how to turn notifications on in the phone's settings | No undrawn screen; the switch itself is the request | **open** |
| 38 | Disabled switches | Not drawn | 40% opacity, like other disabled controls (deviation 1) | The Notifications detail switches are disabled while Reminders is off | **open** |
| 39 | Notification text | Not designed | Phase: title "Your ovulatory phase", body "It’s estimated to start today. See the foods for this phase in Moon Cycle." Period: "Period due in 2 days", "Estimated for Sunday 26 October, based on your dates." Delivered at 9:00 local time | Banners show one title line; estimates are called estimates (`open-questions.md` 11) | **open** |
| 40 | Touch targets under 44 pt (phase selector 40, chips 42, chip × 32, Shop × 40, switches 31, calendar days 42, calendar arrows 40) | Drawn sizes | Unchanged as drawn; each control's touch area reaches 44 pt into the space around it (`ExpandedTapTarget`), so nothing moves | Spec §10: 44 pt targets | **open** |
| 41 | Narrow phones at large text (320 pt, 1.5×) | Not designed | The Home date and the Shop count shrink to fit; the recipe time line and Settings values wrap | No overflow on small phones | **open** |
| 42 | App icon, Android | Adaptive foreground and monochrome on `#FCFAF5` | As handed off, with no extra inset (the generator's default 16% would shrink the mark) | The handoff art is already inside the safe zone | **open** |
| 43 | Launch screen | Background `#FCFAF5` (dark `#1B152E`) | The ring mark centred: plum dot on light, cream dot on dark (the handoff's foreground and splash icons) | Spec §10 | **open** |

How to check a screen against the design: open the Component gallery (debug builds, at the bottom of Settings) and compare with `design/screens/`. Golden images of every component are in `test/goldens/goldens/`.
