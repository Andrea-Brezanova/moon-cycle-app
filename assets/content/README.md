## Foods v2 (content contract)

- Phases feature **35 / 32 / 31 / 44** foods (142 unique); every highlighted food has exactly one featured phase and no repeats. A featured phase is where Moon Cycle introduces or emphasises a food for nutritional fit, meal style and monthly variety; it is not a claim that the food belongs only to that phase.
- Every highlighted food has **`why` copy for its featured phase only**; the app shows `short` on the ingredient sheet when opened from This phase, and no phase rationale from All foods.
- Each phase carries the universal `note`, shown directly below the phase intro on Foods.
- Internal rationale labels (`evidence_informed`, `framework_informed`, `supporting`, `editorial`) and USDA provenance live in the research project (`foods.provenance.json`), never in these files.
- `mayContainAllergens` marks foods that only *may* contain an allergen (oats and gluten, kimchi and fish/shellfish). They are not shown as a permanent allergen, but the avoidance filter still leaves them out for users who avoid that allergen.
- Food ids are permanent. Foods that left the highlighted lists or the searchable catalogue are kept with `shipReady: false` (the app never shows them) so stored shopping items and avoidances still resolve. `kombu` is still used by a recipe and remains visible.

## Rules
- Never rewrite copy here without review. No new health or nutrition claims.
- Ids are permanent: shopping items and avoidances store them.
- Phase placement comes from the reviewed Foods v2 lists; do not edit lists or `why` copy by hand.

## Diet counts: the app's number is the source of truth

Research counts the 112 recipes as vegan 44, vegetarian-only 29, pescatarian-only 28, omnivore 11. The app derives 44 / **28** / **29** / 11, deliberately: `FoodFilter` keeps the conservative rule that a recipe with a food that only *may* contain fish or shellfish (kimchi) is not offered as vegetarian or vegan, because it cannot be called meat-free with confidence. That moves `kimchi-egg-brown-rice-bowl` from vegetarian to pescatarian. The rule is not relaxed to match a target count; `test/content/recipes_v112_test.dart` pins the app's numbers.
